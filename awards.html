Awards & Honours

Empowering Choices Through Research

Celebrating Excellence in Behavioural Research and Education

The Foundation has instituted a distinguished array of awards to recognise and celebrate exceptional contributions in the domains of behavioural research and education. These honours are dedicated to acknowledging individuals who advance the scientific understanding of human behaviour, promote evidence-based educational practices, and bridge rigorous behavioural science with impactful teaching and learning environments.

By highlighting innovation, dedication, and transformative influence, the Foundation aims to foster excellence in behavioural scholarship, pedagogy, and the application of behavioural principles to improve educational outcomes and societal well-being.

The Awards Include:

Prestigious Honour

Lifetime Achievement Awards

These highest honours are conferred upon distinguished scholars, educators, and leaders in behavioural research and education who have demonstrated a sustained, lifelong commitment to advancing the field. Recipients are celebrated for their pioneering theoretical and empirical contributions, enduring influence on behavioural science methodologies, mentorship of future generations, and lasting impact on educational theory, policy, and practice over decades of dedicated service.

Annual Honour

Best Teacher Awards

Presented annually, these awards recognise exceptional educators who excel in applying behavioural principles—such as reinforcement strategies, behaviour management techniques, and evidence-based instructional design—to create highly effective, engaging, and supportive learning environments. Honorees are distinguished by their ability to inspire student motivation, facilitate meaningful behavioural change, promote self-regulation and social-emotional skills, and achieve measurable improvements in academic performance and behavioural outcomes.

Leadership Honour

Distinguished Teacher Awards

This category salutes educators who have achieved sustained excellence and leadership in behavioural education. Awardees are recognised for their deep mastery of behavioural science principles, innovative curriculum development grounded in empirical research, influential mentorship of colleagues and students, and consistent demonstration of pedagogical practices that integrate behaviour analysis, cognitive-behavioural approaches, or related frameworks to foster inclusive, equitable, and high-impact learning experiences.

Special Citation

Best Teacher in Rural Area

This special award highlights the remarkable dedication of educators working in rural and underserved communities, where access to behavioural resources, professional development, and specialised support is often limited. Recipients are honoured for their ingenuity in adapting evidence-based behavioural interventions to resource-constrained settings, overcoming geographic and socioeconomic barriers, addressing diverse behavioural and learning needs, and making profound, positive differences in the educational trajectories and behavioural development of rural students and their communities.

Research Excellence

Distinguished Researchers Awards

These prestigious accolades honour researchers who have produced outstanding, high-impact scholarship in behavioural science with direct or potential applications to education. Awardees are celebrated for their original investigations, rigorous methodological contributions, influential publications, and advancements in applied behaviour analysis, classroom behavioural interventions, motivation and learning theories, social-emotional learning, and evidence-based practices that enhance educational equity, efficacy, and outcomes. Their work inspires continued progress in translating behavioural research into real-world educational improvements.

Through these thoughtfully established awards, the Foundation seeks to cultivate a vibrant culture of excellence, recognise selfless service to behavioural research and education, and motivate ongoing innovation and commitment to using behavioural science to empower learners, educators, and communities worldwide.
