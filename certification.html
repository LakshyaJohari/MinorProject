Certification Programs

Empowering Choices Through Research

Specialised Professional & Academic Skill Development

The Behavioural Research Foundation (BRF) offers specialised certification programs in behavioural finance and allied disciplines for diverse stakeholders—from undergraduate students to postgraduates, PhD scholars, and working professionals.

Program Features

  • Duration: 3–4 days (intensive workshops) to 1 month (in-depth courses)
  • Modes: Online (interactive digital) and offline (in-person)
  • Delivery: Independent BRF programs or joint collaborations with partner organisations (on mutually agreed terms)
  • Credential: Official BRF certificate awarded upon successful completion

Benefits for Trainees

These programs deliver practical, career-enhancing value:

  • Specialised Skills — Gain deep knowledge of behavioural biases, decision-making, prospect theory, and market applications tailored to your level (undergraduate foundation → professional strategy).
  • Credibility Boost — The BRF certificate strengthens resumes, LinkedIn profiles, job applications, promotions, scholarships, and PhD profiles.
  • Flexible Access — Choose online for global convenience or offline for immersive interaction—no career disruption.
  • Networking — Connect with experts, peers, and industry partners (especially in joint programs).
  • Personal & Professional Growth — Improve financial judgment, reduce bias-driven errors, and build confidence in volatile environments.

Benefits for BRF Members

Participation in designing, delivering, or supporting these programs brings direct rewards:

  • Skill Development — Refine teaching, mentoring, research dissemination, and leadership abilities.
  • Professional Visibility — Gain recognition, publication opportunities, speaking roles, and career advancement credits.
  • Expanded Networks — Build alliances with partner organisations, academics, and industry professionals.
  • Organisational Impact — Strengthen BRF’s reputation, attract funding/partnerships, and contribute to mission-driven education.
  • Personal Satisfaction — Experience the fulfilment of shaping future professionals and seeing real-world impact.

BRF certification programs create win-win outcomes: trainees acquire future-ready skills and credentials, while members advance their expertise and influence in behavioural sciences.
