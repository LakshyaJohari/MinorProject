Membership

Empowering Choices Through Research

Join a Dynamic Academic and Professional Community

The Foundation is pleased to offer four thoughtfully designed membership categories, enabling passionate individuals—educators, behavioural researchers, practitioners, students, and supporters—to actively participate in and contribute to the advancement of behavioural research and education.

Membership provides a meaningful way to align with the Foundation's mission of promoting evidence-based practices, fostering innovation in behavioural science applications to learning, supporting high-quality teaching grounded in behavioural principles, recognising excellence through awards, and building a collaborative community dedicated to improving human behaviour, educational outcomes, and societal well-being.

Membership is open to professionals, academics, researchers, teachers, students, and enthusiasts committed to these goals. By becoming a member, individuals gain opportunities to stay connected with cutting-edge developments, engage in networking, participate in Foundation initiatives, access exclusive resources (where applicable), and support the Foundation's award programs, workshops, research dissemination, and community outreach efforts in behavioural science and education.

Membership Options

Annual Subscription

Annual Member

₹500 / year

This flexible, renewable membership is ideal for individuals who wish to engage with the Foundation on an ongoing yearly basis.

Annual Members enjoy active participation in the Foundation's activities for each subscription year, including eligibility to attend events, receive updates on behavioural research and educational advancements, vote in relevant Foundation matters (as per bylaws), and contribute to the collective mission.

Lifetime Affiliation

Life Member

₹2,500 one-time

This prestigious, one-time membership recognises enduring commitment and provides lifelong affiliation with the Foundation.

Life Members receive permanent recognition and sustained involvement, enjoying all privileges of membership throughout their lifetime without the need for annual renewals. This category is especially meaningful for senior educators, established researchers, and committed practitioners.

By Invitation

Honorary Member

Conferred

It is given to persons of repute in this domain of teaching and research.

Distinguished leaders and scholars invited by the Management Committee to provide guidance and intellectual leadership to the Foundation.

Corporate / Institution

Institutional Member

Institutional

The organisation can also be a BRF member.

Enables academic colleges, university departments, research bodies, and corporations to partner formally with BRF for joint workshops, MoUs, and faculty-student exchange.

Through these accessible membership categories, the Foundation seeks to build a broad, inclusive, and vibrant network of stakeholders united by a shared passion for harnessing behavioural research to transform education, empower learners, and create positive behavioural change. Membership contributions directly support the Foundation's core activities, including the institution of awards, organisation of professional development programs, facilitation of research collaborations, and promotion of evidence-informed practices in behavioural education across urban and rural settings.

We warmly invite eligible individuals to join us in this noble endeavor—whether through the affordable annual option or the enduring commitment of life membership—and become valued partners in advancing behavioural research and education for the benefit of current and future generations. To apply, write to us at behaviouralresearchfoundation@gmail.com.
