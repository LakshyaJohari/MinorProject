Digboi College

Partner Institution • Digboi, Tinsukia, Assam

NAAC A+ Grade • UGC Autonomous Status

About the Collaboration

Digboi College, located in the historic oil town of Digboi, Tinsukia district, Assam, is a premier public institution of higher education. Established in 1965, it is affiliated with Dibrugarh University and has been granted autonomous status by the UGC effective from the academic session 2024-25. The college holds an impressive NAAC A+ grade accreditation, reflecting its commitment to academic excellence and quality.

As a provincialised college under the Government of Assam, it serves approximately 2,000 students across 20 departments, offering undergraduate programs in Arts (BA), Science (B.Sc.), Commerce (B.Com), and select postgraduate courses.

Digboi College emphasises holistic development through research, extracurricular activities, NCC, and initiatives like the STAR College scheme for select science departments under RUSA, as well as community and finishing school programs. Situated in a biodiversity-rich region, it fosters environmental awareness and has produced notable alumni in various fields. With modern facilities, a strong faculty, and consistent performance, Digboi College continues to empower students in Upper Assam's educational landscape.
