NAAC B+ Grade

About the Collaboration

Doom Dooma College, located at Rupai Siding in Tinsukia district, Assam, is a prominent general degree college serving the Upper Assam region. Established in 1967 by visionary local pioneers led by Late Jogendra Nath Deka, it was founded to provide accessible higher education amid the area's natural beauty and industrial backdrop.

Affiliated with Dibrugarh University, the college is a provincialised institution under the Government of Assam. It offers Higher Secondary and undergraduate programs in Arts, Science, and Commerce streams (including Honours), with the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) implemented since 2019. Select postgraduate and vocational courses are also available, emphasising holistic education.

Doom Dooma College has earned NAAC accreditation with a B+ grade, reflecting its dedication to quality teaching, infrastructure, and student development. Facilities include a well-equipped computerised library, modern classrooms, and support for extracurricular activities. With a focus on intellectual growth, community engagement, and preparing students for diverse careers, Doomdooma College remains a key educational pillar in Tinsukia district, empowering generations in Northeast India's evolving landscape.
