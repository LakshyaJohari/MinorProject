Jagiroad College

Partner Institution • Jagiroad, Morigaon, Assam

NAAC A+ Grade • UGC Autonomous Status

About the Collaboration

Jagiroad College, located in Jagiroad, Morigaon district, Assam, is a leading multi-faculty public institution committed to quality higher education in a semi-urban setting. Established on August 8, 1979, it was founded by visionary local social workers to address the educational needs of the region. The college is situated just 100 meters from National Highway 37, on a serene 25-bigha campus nestled near the picturesque Sonaikuchi Hill.

Affiliated with Gauhati University, Jagiroad College has achieved significant milestones: it received NAAC A+ grade accreditation and was granted UGC autonomous status effective from June 9, 2025.

As a provincialised college under the Government of Assam, it offers Higher Secondary (Arts & Science under AHSEC), undergraduate programs (BA, B.Sc., B.Com, B.Voc. in areas like Retail Management and Entertainment), and select postgraduate courses, including MTM (Master of Tourism Management) and PGDCA. With four faculties (Arts, Science, Commerce, Vocational), modern facilities, and a focus on holistic development, research, and skill-building, Jagiroad College empowers students in Central Assam's educational ecosystem.
